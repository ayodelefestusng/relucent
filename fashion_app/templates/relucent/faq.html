Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to sizing, international dispatch, returns, and couture care.

For orders within Nigeria: DHL Express delivery takes 1 to 2 business days (Same-day dispatch available in Lagos).

For international clients (United Kingdom, United States, Europe, UAE): DHL Express Worldwide Priority arrives within 3 to 5 business days with full door-to-door tracking.

We feature our proprietary Relucent FitFinder™ AI on every product page. Simply input your height, weight, and preferred silhouette fit to receive an exact tailored recommendation.

We honor a 14-day return window from receipt of your shipment. Garments must be unworn, in original luxury garment sleeves, with security tags fully intact. Size exchanges are complimentary.

We process all major debit/credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Verve, American Express), Apple Pay, Google Pay, Paystack, Flutterwave, and direct bank transfers in NGN, USD, GBP, and EUR.
