2. Returns & Exchanges

We want your Relucent piece to bring you absolute confidence. If you need a size exchange or store return, you have 14 calendar days from the date of delivery.

  • Items must be unworn, unwashed, and without perfume or deodorant marks.
  • All original designer garment tags and security ribbons must remain affixed.
  • Size exchanges within Nigeria and the United Kingdom are complimentary.
