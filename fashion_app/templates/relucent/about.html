The Relucent Manifesto

Where African Grandeur Meets Savile Row Precision

Relucent was conceived as a rebellion against compromise. We craft modern, luxurious, timeless silhouettes designed to turn heads across Lagos, London, New York, and Paris.

01 / The Origin

Crafting Affordable Luxury

For decades, high fashion presented an unfair dichotomy: exorbitant European couture with thousands of percent markup, or fast-fashion synthetic polyester that loses form after two wears.

Relucent Clothing bridges this chasm. We source 100% natural Mulberry silk, double-faced Australian cashmere wools, and Egyptian cottons, cutting and tailoring each garment with Savile Row master cutters.

02 / The Standard

Uncompromising Materiality

Every seam is fortified with french seams and silk bias tape. Hand-finished buttonholes, genuine horn and Mother of Pearl buttons, and bespoke 18k gold-dipped metal aglets are standard on every creation.

"True elegance is when the inside of the garment is as beautiful as the outside."
